# Market and competitor analysis (hypotheses, not measured market share)
## Comparison / differentiating opportunity
| Comparator | What users may already value | Gap to test, not claimed factual deficiency |
|---|---|---|
| FatSecret / MyFitnessPal | food database, diary, macro accounting | quicker saved-meal repeat + cooked-batch recipe workflow |
| Cronometer | detailed nutrition and data quality | reduced complexity for routine tracking |
| YAZIO / Lifesum | approachable visual nutrition UX | mixed-sport + flexible challenges as first-class concepts |
| Strava / Garmin Connect | excellent activity ecosystem | connection to routines and nutrition without requiring intense sport focus |
| Habitica / Streaks / Loop | habit engagement | habit continuity without punitive daily streaks |
## Strategy
Avoid selling 'another calorie counter with AI'. Position on **speed + flexible progress + personal control**. Do not claim comparative superiority until measured in 8–12 user tests.
## Research plan
- 8–12 semistructured interviews: active dieters, meal-preppers, mixed-sport users.
- Time 5 routine tasks in two incumbent apps and in Momentum clickable prototype.
- 14-day dogfood diary: times, failure modes, abandoned tasks, corrections.
- Reassess paid willingness after retention, not before.

## Biggest threats
Catalog accuracy/coverage, data privacy, accidental health claims, Health Connect policy/review, onboarding bloat, unsupported AI accuracy, expensive integrations, offline sync complexity, incumbent switching cost.

## 2026-10-17 gap close (see `18-COMPETITOR-GAPS.md`)
