# Business and financial analysis — assumptions, not observed figures
## Positioning
Fast personal nutrition + mixed-sport + compassionate flexible challenges in one place. Android-first, privacy-first, optional AI.
## Potential model
Free: calorie/macros diary, private recipes, weekly challenges, manual activity. Paid later: optional cloud sync, cross-device, richer analysis/AI, advanced coaching; NEVER paywall export/delete.
## Risks
Competition from large catalogs, expensive data licenses/AI, health data trust, retention burden, localization of foods, misleading photo recognition.
## Test, then price
After 4-week dogfood + 8–12 interviews: ask for demonstrated willingness to pay; test premium usefulness, not sticker-price guess. Keep API model spending capped; forecast by requests/user/month × measured cost/request + EAS/hosting/storage/support and store fees.
## Metrics to unlock commercialization
Day-30 retained meaningful users; repeated logging latency; recipe adoption; voluntary invite referrals; cost per active user; privacy complaints; crash-free sessions.
## Sensitivity template
Total monthly variable cost = active users × (AI requests per user × measured AI cost/request + sync/storage cost/user) + fixed infra. Do not claim margins until measurement.
